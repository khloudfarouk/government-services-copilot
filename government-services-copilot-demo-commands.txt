============================================================ 15.
IMPORTANT DEMO NOTES
============================================================

-   Do NOT commit demo runtime files.
-   data/ is local runtime data.
-   government-service-response.docx is generated output.
-   tsconfig.json has a local intentional modification: “include”:
    [“src/**/*.ts”] Do not change it just for the demo if
    typecheck/tests pass.
-   The demo corpus is synthetic and should be described as an
    official-style sample corpus, not a real government source.
-   The OCR provider is currently a mock provider for the MVP.
-   The retriever is a local keyword-based implementation behind an
    application port; production can replace it with hybrid/vector
    retrieval without changing the application/domain layers.

“Today I am demonstrating an evidence-grounded Government Services

blocks prompt injection, and requires human officer approval before the
response can be finalized and exported.”
